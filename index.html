From her children

She has spent her life taking care of everyone else.

There are people who hold a family together without ever asking to be the center of it. Our mom is that person. The little ones call her Namma Robin. Sophie is 5. Magnus is 1. When they come through the door, she is still the person they run to.

For more than forty years she and Donald have been inseparable — the quiet kind of love that looks like showing up, every day, until showing up is simply who you are. If you know them, you know they belong in the same frame.

Then liver cancer. She went into treatment. And in the middle of that fight, doctors found bone cancer too. Two cancers. One woman. A family trying not to fall apart.

Donald has not left her side. We are asking you not to leave ours.

She is in care at Baylor University Medical Center. The hospital asked for $48,000 for the initial phase of treatment and surgeries — on top of everything the liver cancer already demanded. That number does not include the rest of the road.

We built this page because the bills arrived faster than anyone could catch their breath. We are her children. We are asking friends, church family, neighbors in Mount Vernon, and anyone with a kind heart: please help us give Namma Robin more time with the people who still need her.

Where does the money go?

Every donation goes to Robin’s medical bills — the initial phase of treatment and surgeries, ongoing cancer care at Baylor University Medical Center, and the hospital costs that come with both. Gifts are sent directly to her Venmo, @Robin-Whiteman-3.

Who set up this page?

Robin’s children. This is not a hospital campaign and not a charity with overhead. It is a family asking for help in public, with her name, her photos, and her Venmo.

I do not have Venmo. How can I help?

You can download Venmo and send a gift to Robin-Whiteman-3, ask a family member to send on your behalf, or share this page with someone who can give. Sharing is help.

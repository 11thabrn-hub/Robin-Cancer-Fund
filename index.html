In loving memory

Robin Jo Whiteman

September 27, 2026

A letter from her family

Unfortunately, my mother Robin Jo Whiteman has passed away.

After a short battle with cancer, the doctors at Baylor University determined her vitals could no longer be maintained, and they chose to remove her from the drugs they were using to keep her blood pressure up. Surrounded by her family, we waited until she closed her eyes, which we presumed was for the last time.

To our disbelief, however, her vitals recovered — not just recovered, but she had snapped back and was more lucid than ever. We were amazed and happy that we had more time with our mother. We moved immediately to get her taken home from the hospital as quickly as possible.

Unfortunately, after she was moved home, the damage that was done to her kidneys from the post-surgery treatment was irreversible. On the night of September 27, at 12:07 midnight, my mother passed away in her own bed.

We wanted to thank everyone for their prayers and donations. This has been the most difficult part of our lives, and we appreciate all of the support everyone has given.

Our mother’s last words were to encourage everyone to put their faith in Jesus, get married, and not to hold grudges, because life is too short. In the end, she had made several mentions of seeing heaven and put her faith in Jesus.

Put your faith in Jesus. Get married. Do not hold grudges, because life is too short.

A memorial has been scheduled. If you have any questions, please reach out to us directly.

Thank you again,
The Whiteman Family
